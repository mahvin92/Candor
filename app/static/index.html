About Candor

Candor is an AI-powered cancer mutation intelligence framework developed by ChordexBio to help bridge the gap between complex biological data and informed decision-making.

It was trained on over 2.5 million mutations from The Cancer Genome Atlas (TCGA): 11,160 patients across 33 cancer types. Embedded in Gemini as agentic AI, Candor gives convenient, seamless access to knowledge for interpreting and analyzing large sets of cancer-associated genomic alterations, including for institutions and organizations with limited compute and data-analytics capacity.

By integrating specialized computational analysis with agentic AI, Candor aims to reduce the time needed to access, interpret and synthesize mutational and clinical knowledge. Its collaborative design lets users and AI systems explore genomic profiles together, retrieve relevant insights and translate complex information into decision-support outputs, with applications across medicine, biotechnology, biology, healthcare and the life sciences.

How this prototype works

1 · Gemini understands the request. Gemini on Vertex AI reads your message, recognizes a pasted mutation table and decides when to call Candor. Your table is passed on exactly as pasted: the language model never retypes your data.

2 · Candor profiles the mutations. The Candor engine, a proprietary model running as a private service on Google Cloud, compares the mutation set with TCGA reference cohorts and returns calls for cancer type, disease stage and patient sex, with scores, evidence markers and closest reference profiles.

3 · Gemini explains, and stays honest. Results are shown as exact model output. Gemini explains them, flags weak or ambiguous calls, and asks for genomic coordinates instead of guessing them.

Input format

Gene,Chromosome,Position,Ref>Alt,PatientID with hg38 coordinates, e.g. TP53,chr17,7675088-7675088,C>T,P001. Several patients in one table are pooled into a single profile unless you ask for per-patient results.

Safety

Research prototype only. Matches against public reference data are not a diagnosis and must be reviewed by a qualified clinician. Do not enter real patient data.
